## Platform

web

## Users
- Agency owners and growth operators running outbound for several clients (inferred): they live in this tool for hours a day, switching between client workspaces, scanning contact lists, replying to prospects, checking what sent and what bounced.
- Small teams doing cold email, lead capture and follow-up who want to leave GoHighLevel, Instantly or n8n (stated by the owner as the positioning).
- Self-hosters who evaluate it by opening the landing page, then signing up on their own server (inferred).

## Product Purpose
Orbyt is an open-source (AGPL-3.0), self-hostable growth platform: CRM, cold-email outreach with deliverability controls, a unified inbox (email, SMS, WhatsApp, voice), visual workflows, public forms, a knowledge base with AI chat agents, and agency multi-tenancy. Success is an agency running real client work in it daily instead of GoHighLevel.

## Positioning
The open-source, self-hostable alternative to GoHighLevel + Instantly + n8n in one product, with agency sub-accounts isolated at the database level and bring-your-own AI keys. A hosted competitor cannot truthfully offer "you run it, you own the data, read the code".

## Operating Context
Dense daily-use tool: lists of contacts, sequences, inbox threads, a workflow canvas, settings. Light and dark both plausible (inferred). Used on laptops mostly; must work on a phone for checking replies.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js 16 + Tailwind v4 + shadcn/ui, React Flow for the workflow canvas. Existing routes: contacts, inbox, sequences, workflows, mailboxes, forms, knowledge, deals, tasks, settings, plus sign-in/up and accept-invite. Functionality, routes and data must not change; this is a visual and structural redesign.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles
- Show the real product doing its job (inbox, sequences, workflows), not abstract marketing shapes.
- Dense and fast beats decorative in the app; the brand lives in precise details.

- An agency user must always know which client workspace they are in.
